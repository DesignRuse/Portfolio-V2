02 — About

About Sanjeevv

Sanjeevv Kkumar Dass is a creator of smart digital tools. He builds simple, easy-to-use apps that help entrepreneurs, professionals, creators & students work better, spark new ideas and save time.

His tools are designed to be easy, quick and powerful, without extra fluff, so you can save time, organize work and protect your data. They include PixelFlow Pro, CoinKeeper, SecurePass, SenderPro, WA Bulk Messenger, Cash Tally Pro v22.0, PDFy Studio, PDFMagic, ProfitPulse, ProfitPilot FBA and ProfitCraft™, all available in his Payhip store.

He is also a content creator who turns ideas into words and visuals that people enjoy, sharing stories that educate, inspire and spark creativity, with a focus on keto and healthy living. You can find him on Quora, on Instagram as @leanlifewatch on Pinterest and on Facebook, and he is the owner of two websites: TheKetoWayHub and LeanLife.

His specialties are niche expertise, SEO, storytelling, visual content creation, social media management, audience engagement and data analysis.
